Glossary of Terms

A
Algorithm — A finite sequence of well-defined instructions used to solve a problem or perform a computation.
Abstraction — The process of simplifying complex systems by focusing on essential properties while omitting unnecessary detail.
B
Baseline — A reference value or condition against which subsequent measurements or changes are compared.
C
Convergence — The property of a process or sequence that approaches a definite limit as iterations increase.
Constraint — A limitation or restriction applied to a system, model, or process to bound the solution space.
D
Dependency — A relationship in which one component relies on another for its correct operation or completion.
