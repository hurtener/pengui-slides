What You Should Remember
  1. 01
    Core principle restated concisely

    A one- or two-sentence elaboration that reinforces why this point matters and how it connects to the rest of the document.

  2. 02
    Second most important insight

    Summarise the key finding or method from the body of the document. Keep language active and concrete.

  3. 03
    Third insight or recommended action

    Tie this to a practical implication or next step the reader can take after completing the document.

  4. 04
    Fourth point — optional; remove if not needed

    Limit to four or five takeaways to maintain impact. More than five dilutes the message.
